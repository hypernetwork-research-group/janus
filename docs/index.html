Generate higher-order structure, not just pairwise edges.

Real-world interactions often involve groups of entities at once. Janus learns a distribution of hypergraphs by combining a dual-view variational autoencoder with cross-conditioned latent diffusion, preserving the relationship between nodes and hyperedges.

01

Dual-view representation

Learn separate but interdependent node and hyperedge latent spaces with a β-VAE.

02

Coupled diffusion

Generate two latent views with cross-attention, then decode a coherent incidence structure.

03

Local-to-global generation

Sample local sub-hypergraphs and merge them into realistic larger structures.

How Janus works

1

Decompose the observed hypergraph

Extract overlapping sub-hypergraphs to learn from a single high-order network without modeling the combinatorial hyperedge space directly.

2

Learn two aligned latent spaces

The β-VAE encodes node and hyperedge representations and learns to recover their incidence relations.

3

Diffuse with cross-view conditioning

Paired diffusion transformers denoise both latent streams while exchanging information through cross-attention.

4

Decode and assemble

Decode synthetic sub-hypergraphs and reconcile entities for full hypergraph generation. Janus generates nodes and hyperedges; JanusNC works with a prescribed node set.

Inside the Janus Architecture

Janus combines a dual-view variational autoencoder with cross-conditioned diffusion transformers to jointly model node and hyperedge representations.

A novel Hypergraph Generation Evalutation Framework

Micro

Node degree, hyperedge size and clustering-coefficient distributions.

Meso

Community structure, degree assortativity and open/closed triangles.

Macro

Global size, connectivity, transitivity, overlapness and modularity.

Higher-order

Hyper NetSimile, Hyperedge Portrait Divergence and reconstruction under fixed nodes.

Why cross-attention matters

Removing cross-attention increases Hyper Portrait Divergence from 0.1158 to 0.1924, and Hyper NetSimile from 0.8984 to 1.6320 on the email-Enron ablation. Coupled denoising helps maintain high-order structure.
